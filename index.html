Launching Soon

A child safety app for crowded places — fairs, malls, temples, stations. Most tracking apps watch a child's location constantly. Orbit stays completely silent as long as the child is inside a parent-set safe radius, and only alerts the moment they step outside it. A panic button shares live location for a few minutes, then auto-deletes it. Placed in the top 500 of 4,595 teams at the Omnikon National Hackathon.
